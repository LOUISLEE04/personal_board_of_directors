Feature 01

Deep Research /research

Multi-agent deep dives into any life decision — with real sources, counter-evidence, and a strong verdict.

Smart Dispatch

Classifies the question, dispatches only relevant agents. SOCIAL → emotional-check + pei-local. CAREER → cost-analyst + rd-researcher.

Evidence Standards

Minimum 3 web searches per agent. WebFetch on key sources. All claims cited. Counter-evidence always included.

Output Structure

Verdict → Key Findings → Action Plan → Open Questions → Original Prompt (verbatim). Saved to research/[topic]/

Feature 07

Tactical Planning /tactical

Situation-specific playbooks — exact scripts, timing, and failure modes for specific real-life moments.

Specific, Not Generic

"How do I open the salary negotiation?" gets a real script with exact wording — not "be confident and know your worth."

Social Contract Awareness

Always asks: who initiated this occasion? Flags transactional risk. Never uses someone's goodwill as a platform for a pitch.

Failure Mode Mapping

Lists what goes wrong if you do it wrong — not just what to do. Treats your social instincts as a data point, not a final verdict.

Clarifies Goal First

"What does good look like from this conversation?" — always asked before drafting anything. Outcome shapes the script entirely.

/tactical — salary negotiation first call
⚡ Tactical: Salary Negotiation Call
THE OPENER (exact wording)
"I'm really excited about this role — I do want to discuss the comp package before I sign. Is there flexibility on base?"
WHY THIS WORDING
Leads with enthusiasm — not entitlement. "Is there flexibility" is softer than "I want more" but still opens the door. Never name a number first — let them move first.
FAILURE MODES
✗ Apologising for asking ("sorry to bring this up")
✗ Giving a range — they'll always anchor to the low end
✗ Accepting the first counter without a 24hr think window
✗ Negotiating via email — harder to read tone and respond live
IF THEY PUSH BACK
"I understand — could we look at the equity or signing bonus instead?" Always have a second axis to negotiate on.
